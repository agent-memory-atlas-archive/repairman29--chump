# Every top-level job in .github/workflows/ci.yml that carries
# `continue-on-error: true` AT THE JOB LEVEL posts a check-run to the PR whose
# conclusion is ALWAYS success regardless of what its steps did — a green check
# that carries no information (the R3 "honest ship gate" defect). The default
# stance is: make the job required-and-green or delete it. A job may stay
# continue-on-error ONLY if it is listed here with a one-line decision, and the
# matching row in docs/process/CI_GATES_INVENTORY.md (§ continue-on-error
# PR-reporting jobs) records why.
#
# Format: one job key per line, `#` comments and blank lines ignored. The key is
# the YAML job name under `jobs:` in ci.yml (NOT the display `name:`).
#
# scripts/ci/test-no-coe-pr-reporting.sh fails CI when ci.yml grows a NEW
# job-level continue-on-error job that is not on this list. The list is intended
# to SHRINK: each entry is a decision still owed to the R3 program, not a
# permanent exemption.
